How it reads prices

    

      

        1. A small script runs in the page's main world at document_start and wraps
        WebSocket. Every frame the page receives is copied and relayed to the extension.
      

      

        2. The service worker decodes the frame — JSON first, then a typed-array probe, then a regex
        fallback — and turns it into candles. Frames it cannot decode are kept in the
        Protocol Lab tab so you can see the real payload shape instead of guessing.
      

      

        3. Candles survive service-worker restarts through chrome.storage.session, and
        chrome.alarms restarts the heartbeat every 30 seconds.
      

      

        4. If no broker tab is open, Binance public klines (crypto) and Yahoo (FX, delayed) keep the
        engine warm. Both are labelled in the UI and are never mixed with live broker ticks.
      

    


    
Disclaimer

    

      Q-Sync Pro is a charting and statistics tool. It does not place orders, click buttons, or interact
      with a broker in any way. Binary options are a negative-expectation product for the overwhelming
      majority of participants: at a typical 86% payout you need a 53.8% win rate simply to break even,
      and OTC feeds are synthesised by the platform rather than sourced from an exchange. Use the journal
      and backtester to measure the strategy honestly before you risk a single unit of real money.
